# Getting ready for a first call

The first call is free and takes about 30 minutes. We cover the main pain point, what a good result looks like, and whether I'm the right help. Options come afterward, in writing. If you've asked for an assessment from a [1NEON account](free-accounts.md), I read it first. You can book without one.

## Bring one real example

The last time the problem happened, such as "on Tuesday a referral sat in the inbox for three days," not "our intake is messy."

## Questions to think about

1. What happens, step by step, from start to finish?
2. How often does it happen, and roughly how long does it take?
3. What goes wrong, and what do people do when it does?
4. What must not change, such as a system you're keeping or a way customers reach you?
5. What would "fixed" look like to you?

"About 40 a week, maybe 5 minutes each" is plenty.

## Have nearby, but don't send

- The tools and subscriptions involved
- Who administers each account
- Who else should join, especially whoever does the work every day

Don't send passwords, health details, or payment information. If the work involves health information, we'll go through the [business associate agreement request](../templates/baa-request.md) before any access.

## What you'll leave with

- A plain description of the problem
- Whether I'm a good fit, and if not, who might be
- The next step, if there is one. If the answer is a setting or something you already have, I'll say so

If it makes sense, a short proposal follows. I fill in the [intake form](../forms/intake.md) from our conversation.
