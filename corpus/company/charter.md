# Charter

neon1 is a one-person technology practice in New York, run by me, Pradeep. The company is neon1 LLC, formed in New York on July 24, 2025, and neon1 is the name it does business under. This charter covers why neon1 exists, who it serves, and how it works. The [How I work](../standards/operating-principles.md) documents go into detail.

## Mission

Help people and small organizations get their technology working together and working for them, explained honestly and at a human level, so they're left more capable, confident, and in control than before. [Mission and promise](mission.md) puts it on one page.

## How

I work with cloud and AI software, infrastructure, and connected devices, and use them where they make the result better. For each tool or system in an engagement, the recommendation is one of seven outcomes:

| Outcome | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Keep** | It works. Leave it alone, maybe with a note on how it's used |
| **Configure** | The tool you have can already do it. Turn on what's there |
| **Integrate** | Connect it to the other tools it should share information with |
| **Migrate** | Move it somewhere that fits better, with your data intact |
| **Replace** | Swap it for something that does the job with less effort |
| **Retire** | Stop paying for it and shut it down cleanly |
| **Outsource** | Someone else is better placed to run it, and I'll say so |

Moving everything to the cloud, or to an AI model, is never the default answer.

## Values, as behavior

- **Listen first.** I write down the real workflow before proposing anything.
- **Simplicity.** I pick the option with the smallest ongoing burden that still meets the need.
- **Quality.** The result works, can be recovered when it breaks, and you can run it.
- **Transparency.** Scope, price, evidence, and limits are clear before work starts.
- **Your independence.** You own your accounts and billing. You get the docs, the exports, and a clean way out.
- **Reuse before building.** Existing tools and open-source software come first. I build only the part nothing else covers.

## Who I serve first

1. **Owner-operated service businesses and small practices** losing time between email, spreadsheets, point of sale, documents, and apps that don't talk to each other.
2. **Technical small businesses** that need clarity on their cloud accounts, repeatable deployments, and recovery.
3. **Individuals, homes, and connected devices**, when the travel, safety, and upkeep make sense.

## What I refer to others

Some work needs a specialist or a team with round-the-clock coverage. For these, I'll refer you to someone who fits:

- 24/7 emergency support
- Safety-critical equipment
- Regulated professional judgment, such as legal, medical, or accounting advice
- Large migrations that need a team
- Formal security assurance, such as a penetration test or an audit opinion

## Free help

Paid work is paid. Separately, I set aside a little time each month for community help, chosen at my discretion. That's a habit, not a claim to be a nonprofit.
