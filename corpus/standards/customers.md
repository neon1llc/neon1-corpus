# Customers

Some things stay the same for every customer: I listen first, say plainly what I can and can't do, keep you in control of what you own, and leave you able to run it. The shape of the work changes with the kind of customer.

## Everyone

- No shaming an old spreadsheet, a cheap tool, or anyone's comfort level with technology.
- You see the task done, then you do it, then you fix a harmless mistake yourself.
- Monitoring, recording, AI use, and data access are always visible. Efficiency never becomes hidden surveillance of your staff.
- "Do nothing," "use the support you already have," and "hire a specialist" are always on the table.
- Your accounts, data, and documents stay yours, with a clean way out.

## Households and individuals

Home networks, cameras, devices, passwords, and security habits for families. I write steps in plain words, slow down when asked, and think about who will help when I'm not there. Embarrassment about technology is never a sales tactic. For older relatives, a printed one-page guide often beats an app.

## Small businesses and practices

My main focus. Owner-run service businesses, offices, and practices where the same information gets typed into several places, nobody quite understands the setup, or the person who knew how it worked has left. A first engagement is usually a paid review or one workflow, small enough to finish and check in a few weeks. I talk with both the owner who pays and the person who does the work.

## Technical teams and B2B

Startups and technical small businesses that need clearer cloud accounts, deployments they can trust, infrastructure as code, AI assistants grounded in their own documents, or SOC 2 readiness. Here I work inside your repositories and processes, follow your conventions, and document decisions as I go. Your team owns the result and can keep going without me.

## Larger organizations and enterprises

I'm most useful on one focused piece of the effort, such as a proof of concept, a review, an automation, or training, alongside your own teams. I'll follow your security, procurement, and change processes. I won't take on round-the-clock operations, large migrations that need a team, or formal assurance. The [alternatives page](../landscape/where-else-to-get-help.md) lists who's better placed for those.

## Health care and other regulated work

When a project touches health information, a [business associate agreement](../templates/business-associate-agreement.md) is signed before any access, and the work is designed to use as little sensitive data as possible. I don't make clinical, legal, or financial judgments, and I don't build systems that make them unattended.

## When I say no

I'll turn down or refer work that needs emergency coverage, safety-critical equipment, regulated professional judgment, hidden employee monitoring, or anything I can't support properly. I'll also say no when what you need is simpler than what you asked for, and point you to the simpler thing.

## Community help

Separately from paid work, I set aside a little time each month to help people and groups in my community, chosen at my discretion. It isn't used to sell paid work.
