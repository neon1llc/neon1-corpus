# How 1NEON accounts work

[1NEON](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/) is the portal my clients use. You can start in it before we talk: outline your project, then ask for an assessment of up to three projects, or book the first call. I read what you wrote first. No card, no password.

[Create an account](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/?signup=1)

## How it goes

1. **Sign up** with your email, your name, which describes you best, and your organization's name if you have one. A website or location is optional. A code emailed to you finishes it.
2. **Outline a project.** **Next step** on Home and the project's **Overview** show six optional parts to write, with a tip for each missing one. [What to write](#what-to-write) shows what a good answer looks like.
3. **Ask for an assessment** from **Next step**: choose one to three projects and add a required note of a sentence or two. I reply by email with a likely approach and the first questions to settle. It isn't a design, a proposal, or the paid [assessment](../engagement/index.md) with costed next steps. It goes to me 30 minutes after your last change, so you can edit or withdraw it until then.
4. **We talk,** if you like. Message me any time in the chat (the round Neo button, then **neon1**), or talk for 30 minutes: book from **Next step**, ask me to email you, or use **Contact neon1** in the menu. Options come afterward, in writing.
5. **If it fits,** we agree on the work and your account becomes a client account. Everything you wrote stays, and the outline becomes the plan.

I work remotely across the US, and on site within about 30 miles of Queens, NY. Outside the US, ask.

## What to write

An outline is what you write down about a project before I assess it. Every part is optional, and a short answer is enough. A part counts when it holds real words, about a dozen characters or more, and an unanswered starter question doesn't count. A project is outlined with the first three parts and fully outlined with all six.

| Part | Write | Where it goes once the work starts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What it is** | The project in a sentence or two: the summary on the Overview. | The scope in the proposal. |
| **How it works today** | One real example of how it works today, answered in Needs. | The starting point Observe measures against. |
| **What done looks like** | What will be true when it's done: the outcome on the Overview. | The checks in Verify that say it's finished. |
| **Who and what it touches** | Who does this work and the tools it touches: an answered question or a system added in Needs. | The handover in Normalize: accounts, access, and training. |
| **How we'll know it worked** | A measure with where it is now, added in Observe. | Tracked in Observe, before and after. |
| **Timing and limits** | A target date, or limits such as budget or must-nots. | The proposal's schedule and constraints. |

### What a good answer looks like

These are made up, for a building-supply yard.

- **What it is:** "Turn phone and email quote requests into one queue, so none are missed."
- **How it works today:** "On Tuesday a contractor's email for 40 sheets of drywall sat unseen for three days."
- **What done looks like:** "Every request gets a quote within one business day."
- **Who and what it touches:** "Two counter staff and the owner. Email, the phone line, and the inventory spreadsheet."
- **How we'll know it worked:** "Quote time, now about 2 days."
- **Timing and limits:** "Before the spring rush, with nothing that changes the till."

## How long it lasts

Home shows the date your account is kept until. If nothing changes, you get reminders at 7 and 14 days, a final notice at 21, and at 30 days the account and what's in it are deleted. **Keep it 30 more days** on Home (once) if you need more time, and **Download a copy** any time. While an outline is unfinished and nothing is waiting for me, you also get outline tips by email about 3 and 7 days after your last change. Turn them off under Outline tips in your profile's Notifications.

Section 3 of the [terms of use](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/terms) covers these accounts, and the [privacy and data policy](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/privacy) covers what sign-up collects, including Cloudflare's invisible check that you're a person. I don't add you to a mailing list.
