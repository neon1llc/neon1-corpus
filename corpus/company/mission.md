# Mission and promise

## Mission

Design, build, and improve technology that does a defined job well, and leave the people who own it fully in control of it.

## The promise

I'm Pradeep. neon1 is my independent engineering and technology consulting practice, for businesses, technical teams, and individuals. You can count on five things:

- **A clear recommendation.** What will help, what it costs to build and run, and what it won't do, before you decide. Sometimes that's a product you already have, or leaving things as they are.
- **Scope you approve.** Deliverables, acceptance criteria, and price agreed before work starts, and changed only with your approval.
- **Tested work.** Built securely, tested against what we agreed, and recoverable when something fails.
- **Ownership stays with you.** Accounts, data, code, and documentation are yours, with a clean way out at any time.
- **A handover that holds.** Documentation and a walkthrough for whoever runs it next. Ongoing care if you want it.
