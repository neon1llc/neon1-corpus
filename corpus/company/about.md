# About neon1

I'm [Pradeep](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pradeep-tathineni/), an engineer with about 15 years of building and running systems. Recent work includes a [document assistant for an accounting firm](../case-studies/answers-in-seconds.md), a [patient intake portal](../case-studies/intake-out-of-the-inbox.md), and [two offices moved off local servers](../case-studies/two-offices-off-local-servers.md).

neon1 LLC is my independent practice, registered in New York in July 2025 ([the state record](registration.md)). I take a few clients at a time and do the work myself, so the person you talk to is the person who builds it. [When I work and what I don't take on](../faq/working-with-neon1.md) is spelled out, and [contact](contact.md) is the way to start.
