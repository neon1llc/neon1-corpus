# Where I learn from

## Writing and documentation

- **[Plain language guide series](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language)**, Digital.gov, which now hosts the federal plain language guidelines
- **[Writing to GOV.UK standards](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/)**, UK Government Digital Service
- **[How users read on the web](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-users-read-on-the-web/)**, Nielsen Norman Group
- **[Google developer documentation style guide](https://developers.google.com/style)**, Google
- **[The Chicago Manual of Style](https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/home.html)**, University of Chicago Press
- **[Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/)**, Daniele Procida
- **[Write the Docs guide](https://www.writethedocs.org/guide/)**, the Write the Docs community

## Security and privacy

- **[NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework)**, US National Institute of Standards and Technology
- **[Small business cybersecurity](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber)**, NIST
- **[Small organisations guide to cyber security](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/small-organisations-guide-to-cyber-security)**, UK National Cyber Security Centre
- **[CIS Controls](https://www.cisecurity.org/controls)**, Center for Internet Security
- **[Digital identity guidelines, SP 800-63B](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html)**, NIST
- **[Passkeys](https://fidoalliance.org/passkeys/)**, FIDO Alliance
- **[Application Security Verification Standard](https://asvs.owasp.org/)** and the **[cheat sheet series](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/)**, OWASP
- **[Threat modeling](https://community.owasp.org/Threat_Modeling)**, OWASP, and the **[Threat Modeling Manifesto](https://www.threatmodelingmanifesto.org/)**
- **[NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework)**, NIST
- **[Business associates](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html)**, US Department of Health and Human Services

## Reliability and recovery

- **[Site Reliability Engineering](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/)** and **[The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/)**, Google
- **[Reliability pillar](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/reliability-pillar/welcome.html)** of the **[AWS Well-Architected Framework](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/well-architected/)**, Amazon Web Services
- **[Contingency planning guide, SP 800-34](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/upd1/final)**, NIST
- **[Ransomware guide](https://www.cisa.gov/stopransomware/ransomware-guide)**, US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency
- **[Incident management handbook](https://www.atlassian.com/incident-management/handbook)**, Atlassian

## Building and shipping software

- **[The Twelve-Factor App](https://12factor.net/)**, Adam Wiggins and contributors at Heroku
- **[Secure Software Development Framework, SP 800-218](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final)**, NIST
- **[Code review guidelines](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/)**, Google
- **[The practical test pyramid](https://martinfowler.com/articles/practical-test-pyramid.html)**, Ham Vocke on Martin Fowler's site
- **[DORA research](https://dora.dev/)**, DevOps Research and Assessment
- **[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/)**, **[Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/en/v1.0.0/)**, and **[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/)**
- **[Pro Git](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2/Git-Branching-Branches-in-a-Nutshell)**, Scott Chacon and Ben Straub
- **[Documenting architecture decisions](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions)**, Michael Nygard

## AI

- **[AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)** and its **[generative AI profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf)**, NIST
- **[Top 10 for LLM applications](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/)**, OWASP

## Design and accessibility

- **[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)** and the **[introduction to accessibility](https://www.w3.org/WAI/fundamentals/accessibility-intro/)**, W3C Web Accessibility Initiative
- **[Ten usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/)** and **[usability testing 101](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/usability-testing-101/)**, Nielsen Norman Group
- **[GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual)** and **[Design System](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/)**, UK Government Digital Service
- **[Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)**, Google

## Improving how work gets done

- **[Lean lexicon](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/muda-mura-muri/)**, Lean Enterprise Institute
- **[The Kanban guide](https://kanban.university/kanban-guide/)**, Kanban University
- **[e-Handbook of Statistical Methods](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/)**, NIST and SEMATECH
- **[FinOps Framework](https://www.finops.org/framework/)**, FinOps Foundation
