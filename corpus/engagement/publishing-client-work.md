# Publishing client work

I publish client work only with appropriate permission and evidence supporting what I say. Information about the industry, year, project, and results is included only when it does not identify the client without permission.

Named references, quotations, and identifying material require separate approval. Results are described according to the evidence available, including any relevant limitations.

Your choices are in the [service agreement](service-agreement.md#case-studies).
