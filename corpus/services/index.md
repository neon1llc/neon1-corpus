# Services

Most of my work is for owner-run businesses: the orders, intake, invoices, and records that get typed into more than one place, or that live in one person's head. A typical first project connects one of those flows end to end in **2 to 4 weeks**, with the steps written down for your staff.

| What's going on | A similar project |
|---|---|
| We type the same orders, forms, or invoices into more than one place | [Intake, out of the shared inbox](../case-studies/intake-out-of-the-inbox.md) |
