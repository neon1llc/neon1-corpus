# Where else to get help

Different needs call for different help. These are the main options, how they usually charge, and when each serves you better. Prices change, so check each provider.

| Option | Best for | Usually charges | Better than neon1 when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doing it yourself, with vendor support | One task inside a tool you already use | Included in your subscription | The product's own features and help cover it |
| Free advising: [SCORE's free mentoring](https://www.score.org/how-mentoring-works/), [NY Small Business Development Centers](https://www.nysbdc.org/), public libraries such as the [New York Public Library](https://www.nypl.org/) | Planning and an independent second opinion | Free | You're still deciding what to do, or need business advice beyond technology |
| Freelance marketplaces: [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/pricing/client), [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/) | A narrow, well-defined build | The freelancer's price plus platform fees: Upwork adds 5% (Basic) or 10% (Business Plus) and a contract fee. Fiverr adds a fee at checkout | You can write the specification and check the result yourself |
| Managed service providers (MSPs) | Help desk, devices, and security for a team | A monthly fee per user or device | Your staff need a help desk or someone to answer at 2 a.m. |
| Retail and repair shops, such as Best Buy's [Geek Squad](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/electronics/services/pcmcat1528819595254.c?id=pcmcat1528819595254) | A broken device or a home install | A price per job, or a membership that adds 24/7 support | A device needs parts, a repair, or a walk-in visit |
| Agencies | Larger builds that need a team | Project fees or retainers | The work needs several people at once, for months |
| Fractional CTO or IT director | Technology leadership for a growing company | A monthly retainer | You need a leader in your meetings every week |
| Large consultancies | Enterprise programs | Large contracts | You need scale, formal methods, or audit services |
| Security specialists: MSSPs, auditors, penetration testers | Monitoring, audits, formal testing | Retainers or fixed engagements | You need an audit opinion, a penetration test report, or 24/7 monitoring |

## Things to watch for

- **Freelancers:** quality, handover, and documentation vary. Make sure accounts are in your name.
- **MSPs:** long contracts, tools and accounts in the provider's name, and exit fees. Ask how you'd leave before signing. Provider-written 2026 pricing guides mostly put small-business plans around $100 to $250 per user per month.
- **Repair shops:** they fix the device in front of them, rarely how your tools work together.

## Where neon1 fits

Owner-run businesses, small practices, and households that want their tools working together, and to understand and own the result, documented at handover. neon1 works by appointment with at most two clients at a time, so it isn't the choice for 24/7 coverage, a large team, formal assurance, or regulated professional judgment.

If you're not sure, ask, and I'll point you elsewhere when someone fits better.
