# Compliance basics for small businesses

*Public · v0.1.5*

**Compliance rules explained by domain, with training.** Open guides for the owner, office manager, or IT lead who has to work out what a rule asks and train staff on it, without a compliance department. It's open on [GitHub](https://github.com/neon1llc/compliance-basics).

- **Who it's for:** small and midsize businesses and the vendors who serve them, plus trainers and consultants who want open material to teach from.
- **How it's laid out:** a one-page overview you can read in 20 seconds, a walkthrough of each rule that takes 20 minutes or less (who it applies to, what it asks, what staff need to know, and the penalties), and a 20-day learning plan for each domain, one task a day. The 20 days are a learning pace, not a deadline.
- **What it covers:** healthcare (HIPAA), life sciences (GxP and Part 11), payments (PCI DSS), financial services (the FTC Safeguards Rule), education (FERPA and COPPA), privacy (state privacy laws and New York's SHIELD Act), security (the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, CIS Controls, and SOC 2), accessibility (ADA and WCAG), marketing (CAN-SPAM and TCPA), and AI use (the NIST AI RMF and NYC Local Law 144).
- **Training kit:** a facilitator guide for self-study, a 30-minute session, or a workshop, an acknowledgment form, and a training log. Each walkthrough ends with quiz questions.
- **SOC 2:** the walkthrough here explains the report in brief. [SOC 2 readiness, step by step](soc-it-2-me.md) is the full course.
- **Licenses:** written material under CC BY-SA 4.0, code under MIT, and the names reserved.

## Current limits

It explains rules in plain words and points to the official text. It covers federal rules and a few New York and state rules, not every state. Each page shows when it was last reviewed, and rules change after that.
