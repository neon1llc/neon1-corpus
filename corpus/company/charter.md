# Charter

neon1 is my independent engineering and technology consulting practice in New York, formed as neon1 LLC ([registration](registration.md)). Detail is in [How I work](../standards/operating-principles.md).

## Mission

See [Mission and promise](mission.md).

## How

I use cloud and AI software, infrastructure, and connected devices where they make the result better. Each tool or system gets one of seven recommendations:

| Outcome | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Keep** | It works. Leave it alone, maybe with a note on how it's used |
| **Configure** | The tool you have can already do it. Turn on what's there |
| **Integrate** | Connect it to the other tools it should share information with |
| **Migrate** | Move it somewhere that fits better, with your data intact |
| **Replace** | Swap it for something that does the job with less effort |
| **Retire** | Stop paying for it and shut it down cleanly |
| **Outsource** | Someone else is better placed to run it, and I'll say so |

Moving everything to the cloud, or to an AI model, is never the default answer.

## Values, as behavior

- **Understand first.** I learn the goal, the systems, and the constraints before proposing anything.
- **Simplicity.** I pick the option with the smallest ongoing burden that still meets the need.
- **Quality.** The result works, can be recovered when it breaks, and you can run it.
- **Transparency.** Scope, price, evidence, and limits are clear before work starts.
- **Your independence.** You own your accounts and billing. You get the docs, the exports, and a clean way out.
- **Reuse before building.** Existing tools and open-source software come first. I build only the part nothing else covers.

## Who I work with

Businesses, technical teams, and individuals with a defined goal, described by their situation rather than their size. [Customers](../standards/customers.md) has the detail, and [services](../engagement/index.md) lists the four kinds of work. Larger organizations get one focused piece of work, inside their own security, procurement, and change processes.

## Who I bring in

Some work is best done with a specialist beside me. [When someone else fits better](../standards/customers.md#when-someone-else-fits-better) lists which.

## Community help

Paid work is paid. Separately, I set aside a little time each month for community help, at my discretion. It's a habit, not a claim to be a nonprofit.

## Products

neon1 also builds public tools and publishes teaching material, each with its own license and release status. None is sold as a service until it can be supported like one.

## Related companies

N1 Supply LLC is a separate company for product experiments. It shares an owner with neon1 LLC but not its money, contracts, or intellectual property, unless a written agreement says otherwise.
