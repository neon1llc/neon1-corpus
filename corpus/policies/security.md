# Security disclosure

*Last updated November 9, 2026.*

If you think you've found a security problem in neon1.tech, the 1NEON portal, Neo, or any [neon1llc repository](https://github.com/neon1llc), thank you. Please tell me privately so it can be fixed before anyone else finds it.

## How to report

Email [security@neon1.tech](mailto:security@neon1.tech). If that bounces, use [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech). Please don't open a public issue. Helpful details include:

- What you found and where (the URL, repository, or file)
- Steps to reproduce it
- What someone could do with it
- How you'd like to be credited, if at all

## What happens next

- I'll acknowledge your report within a few days.
- I'll keep you updated while I investigate and fix it.
- Once it's fixed, I'm glad to credit you publicly if you'd like.
- There's no bug bounty. neon1 is a one-person company, and I can't offer payment.

## Please don't

- Access, change, or delete other people's data, including 1NEON workspaces that aren't yours.
- Run automated scans that put real load on the site, Neo, or the portal.
- Try phishing, social engineering, or physical attacks.
- Share the problem publicly before it's fixed.

## In return

If you act in good faith and follow this policy, I won't pursue legal action over your research, and I'll work with you to understand and fix the problem.

## Scope

In scope: neon1.tech and its subpaths, including the 1NEON portal and Neo's chat, and the public neon1llc repositories. Out of scope: third-party services neon1 uses, such as Amazon Web Services, GitHub, Google, and Calendly. Please report those to their owners.
