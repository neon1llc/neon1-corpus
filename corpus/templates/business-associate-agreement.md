# Business associate agreement

> [!warning] An example, not legal advice
> This is an example to show what neon1 LLC agrees to when a project involves protected health information. It follows the structure of the sample provisions published by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, which says using its sample isn't required for compliance. Your agreement should be reviewed by your own counsel and fitted to your practice, your state, and the actual services. Where a signed agreement differs from this example, the signed agreement governs.

This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is made on ____ ("Effective Date") between ____ ("Covered Entity") and neon1 LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Business Associate"), together the "Parties."

## Background

Covered Entity and Business Associate have entered, or plan to enter, an agreement for the services described in ____ (the "Services Agreement"). In providing those services, Business Associate may create, receive, maintain, or transmit Protected Health Information on Covered Entity's behalf. The Parties enter this Agreement to meet the requirements of the HIPAA Rules.

## 1. Definitions

**Catch-all.** Terms used but not defined here, such as Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Health Care Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Notice of Privacy Practices, Required By Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information, and Use, have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules.

**HIPAA Rules** means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification, and Enforcement Rules at 45 CFR Part 160 and Part 164.

**Protected Health Information** ("PHI") has the meaning in 45 CFR 160.103, limited to the information Business Associate creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on Covered Entity's behalf.

## 2. Obligations of Business Associate

Business Associate agrees to:

1. Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this Agreement or as Required By Law.
2. Use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 for electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure other than as this Agreement provides.
3. Report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure not provided for by this Agreement that it becomes aware of, including Breaches of Unsecured PHI as 45 CFR 164.410 requires, and any Security Incident it becomes aware of, within ____ days of discovery. Unsuccessful attempts that don't result in unauthorized access, such as blocked sign-in attempts and routine network scans, are reported in aggregate on request.
4. Ensure, under 45 CFR 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on its behalf agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions, and requirements.
5. Make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity, or as Covered Entity directs, within ____ days of a request, so Covered Entity can meet 45 CFR 164.524.
6. Make any amendment to PHI in a Designated Record Set that Covered Entity directs or agrees to under 45 CFR 164.526, within ____ days.
7. Keep and provide, within ____ days, the information Covered Entity needs to give an accounting of disclosures under 45 CFR 164.528.
8. To the extent it carries out an obligation of Covered Entity under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164, comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to Covered Entity in performing that obligation.
9. Make its internal practices, books, and records available to the Secretary for determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

## 3. Permitted uses and disclosures

1. Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the services in the Services Agreement.
2. Business Associate may use or disclose PHI as Required By Law.
3. Business Associate agrees to make uses, disclosures, and requests for PHI consistent with Covered Entity's minimum necessary policies and procedures.
4. Business Associate may not use or disclose PHI in a way that would violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except as allowed in items 5 and 6.
5. Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities.
6. Business Associate may disclose PHI for those purposes only if the disclosure is Required By Law, or if Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurances from the recipient that the information will stay confidential, be used or further disclosed only as Required By Law or for the purpose it was disclosed, and that the recipient will notify Business Associate of any known breach of confidentiality.
7. Business Associate will not provide Data Aggregation services, sell PHI, or use PHI for marketing.

## 4. Obligations of Covered Entity

Covered Entity will notify Business Associate of:

1. Any limitation in its Notice of Privacy Practices under 45 CFR 164.520, to the extent it may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.
2. Any change in, or revocation of, an Individual's permission to use or disclose their PHI, to the extent it may affect Business Associate.
3. Any restriction on use or disclosure Covered Entity has agreed to or must follow under 45 CFR 164.522, to the extent it may affect Business Associate.

Covered Entity will not ask Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any way that wouldn't be allowed under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity.

## 5. How neon1 handles PHI in practice

These commitments describe how Business Associate meets Section 2 for typical neon1 work. They add to the obligations above and don't narrow them.

- PHI is accessed only in systems Covered Entity owns or approves, with named accounts, multi-factor sign-in, and the least access the work needs.
- PHI isn't copied to personal devices, email, chat, or development environments. Development and testing use synthetic or de-identified data.
- PHI is never entered into an AI service unless Covered Entity approves that service in writing and the service is covered by its own business associate agreement.
- Access is removed when the services end or are no longer needed, and its removal is confirmed in the handover record.
- A Subcontractor is used only with Covered Entity's written approval.

## 6. Term and termination

1. **Term.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and ends on ____ or when Covered Entity terminates it for cause, whichever comes first.
2. **Termination for cause.** If Covered Entity determines that Business Associate has violated a material term, Covered Entity may terminate this Agreement and the Services Agreement if Business Associate doesn't cure the violation within ____ days of written notice.
3. **On termination.** Business Associate will return to Covered Entity, or with Covered Entity's written agreement destroy, all PHI it still holds in any form, keep no copies, and confirm this in writing. If returning or destroying some PHI isn't feasible, Business Associate will keep it only for the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, continue to protect it under this Agreement, and not use or disclose it for any other purpose.
4. **Survival.** Business Associate's obligations under item 3 survive termination.

## 7. General

1. **Regulatory references.** A reference to a section of the HIPAA Rules means the section as in effect or as amended.
2. **Amendment.** The Parties will amend this Agreement as needed for compliance with the HIPAA Rules and other applicable law.
3. **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity is resolved in favor of a meaning that complies with the HIPAA Rules.
4. **No third-party beneficiaries.** Nothing here gives rights to anyone other than the Parties.
5. **Notices.** Notices go to the addresses below, or to an address a Party later gives in writing.
