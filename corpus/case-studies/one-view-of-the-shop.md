# Operations integration and monitoring

## The problem

A small business ran on systems that didn't know about each other. To see whether the business was on track, the owner had to be there, or open each system in turn.

The ask: see what's happening, from the floor or from home, without one more screen for staff to look after.

## The work

I kept each system as the record for what it does best and added only what joins them.

- A small AWS service ties events from the separate systems together.
- Wall tablets show the business's state, and the owner sees the same view remotely after signing in.
- If one system stops answering, the others keep working and the view says what's out of date.

## The results

The owner can see and act on problems without being on site.

## After handover

I manage the setup remotely, and the owner and staff use it day to day. Computer vision is in progress, and it gets tested before anyone relies on it.
