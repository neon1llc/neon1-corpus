# Ready, Secure, Go

*Private · pre-release*

**Security readiness for every space.** Plain-language guidance for a phone, a home network, a classroom, a small office, or a company heading into SOC 2: five things worth doing first, then what's next.

It includes a five-minute readiness check, practice cards by level, playbooks for when something goes wrong, teach kits, and branded PDF and Word handouts. It was called neon1 Ready until November 2026.
