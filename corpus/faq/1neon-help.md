# FAQs

Short answers about using 1NEON. Signed in? Tap **?** at the top right of any page for help with that page.

## Getting started

### What is 1NEON?
The portal where we run your projects together, with what you need, the work, the results, and the handover in one place. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live).

### Can I try it first?
Yes, three ways, with no account. The [guided walkthrough](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live) plays a fictional project on the real portal in about three minutes (the full story takes about 17), with controls to pause, pick a chapter, or exit. The [interactive demo](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/demo/) is a working copy with made-up clients; your changes stay in your browser until **Start over**. The [sample project](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/example) is one made-up project to read, from request to handover. Short walkthroughs show one thing each: [joining from an invitation](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite), [adding a passkey](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey), [adding an authenticator app](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=authenticator), and [texts and text codes](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=texts).

### How do I get in?
You need an invite. Open its link, enter your email, and sign in with an emailed code or a passkey. No password needed. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

### I didn't get the sign-in code.
Check spam or promotions, then wait a minute and choose **Send a new code**. Codes work for a few minutes, in the browser that asked. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

### Is there a tour?
Yes, offered at your first sign-in. Skip it or ask to be reminded, and replay it from **?** or your profile. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

## Projects and the four layers

### What are Needs, Execute, Observe, and Normalize?
The four layers every project moves through. **Needs** is what you want and the options to get there, **Execute** the work, **Observe** the results against the goals, and **Normalize** what makes it last: handover, training, and care. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live).

### What should I do first on a project?
Look at **Next step** at the top. It says what has to happen now and who it's waiting on, and moves on as soon as that's done. When nothing is waiting, it names the stage's goal and offers **Move to** the next stage. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=propose).

### What do Not assessed, On track, At risk, and Off track mean?
They're the project's health, someone's assessment of how it's going. A new project is **Not assessed** until the team sets it, on the overview or with a status update; starting work or moving stages doesn't change it. The overview shows who set it and when. Items have their own status: To do, In progress, Blocked, or Done. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What if we rename, reorder, or hide stages?
Projects follow the new names and order. A hidden stage still shows while a project is in it, and under any name, Decide still asks for an approved option and Closed still ends a project.

### How do I tell you what I need?
On the project's **Needs** tab, tap **Add request** and finish a sentence with a few taps: what you'd like to do (or **+** for your own), what it's for, what happens now, how often, and when it's due. **Define another need** adds more, each its own request. Only the first choice is required. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=needs).

### Do I have to write a lot?
No. Each section shows what belongs there, an example, and one-tap suggestions. Discovery questions take a short answer where they're asked, and a real example helps most. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=start).

### How do I know when something changes?
The bell glows when something new happens, like an upload, a decision, or a stage change. Tap it to see what and who, and tap one to open it. Important changes also email you; choose which under **Notifications** in your profile. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=listen).

### How do I comment or change an item?
Tap its title. A panel opens with its status, owner, due date, details, and comments, which your whole organization sees. Options and measures open the same way. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### How do I check something off?
Tick the box beside it; counts on each tab show how many are done. A choice between options counts as one, done once one is approved, and a measure is done at its target. Status updates, decisions, and uploaded files don't count, but a **File needed** does, ticked when you send it. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=handover).

### How do I delete something?
Use the item's trash button or **Delete** in its panel; both ask first. Approved options and decisions can't be deleted, since they record what was agreed. Files can be deleted by their uploader and admins.

### What does the onion next to a project mean?
Progress. Needs is the center ring and Normalize the outside, each filling as its work gets done.

### What does the board show?
Every project as a card in its stage, from the first call to care after handover. Drag a card to move it, or use **Move to** on a phone. A closed project moves back the same way, or with **Reopen**. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What does the timeline show?
Under **Projects**, **Timeline** draws each project from start to target date, colored by layer: the dashed line is today, the diamond the target, the lighter part time ahead, and red stripes time past the target. Each row says its stage and days left. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=care).

### How do I start a project?
Choose **New project** and answer a few questions; it starts with a fitting checklist you can change later. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=start).

### What is the calendar on Home?
A month at a time. The arrows step months, **Today** comes back, and the month list jumps from a year back to two ahead. Marks show due dates, milestones, project targets, and your organization's events; choose a day to see them. Editors add leave, closures, meetings, or care plan dates, for one day or several, timed or all day, repeating weekly or monthly. Below, **Overdue** and **Upcoming** start open and **Past** and **Repeated** folded, and leave in the next week shows under **Away this week**. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=calendar).

### What is Insights?
Charts from your organization's data: open and overdue work, on-time finishes, days to a decision, work finished per week, projects across the layers, and their health. Work counts as finished the day it was marked done (the latest, if reopened). The page explains each number. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=observe).

## Proposals, decisions, and signing

### What is a proposal revision?
neon1's options are issued together as a numbered proposal, such as 0039-r1, each option with its own ID, such as 0039-r1-B. Issuing freezes a copy, so what you approve is exactly what you read. Changed options come in the next revision (0039-r2), and earlier ones stay readable, marked as replaced. Each revision shows when its prices were checked; past the recheck date, approval waits for a refresh. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=propose).

### How do I approve an option?
On the **Needs** layer, compare the options and choose **Approve this option**. Add any conditions, such as "only after 6pm", then type your full name and tick the box; your typed name is your signature. The approval lists the option's terms again before you sign (its ID, description, price and whether it's fixed or an estimate, hours, rate, timeline, outside costs, and purchases) and records them exactly as shown, with your name and the time. To ask for a change, comment on the option or send a marked-up proposal. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Can I download the proposal?
Yes. **Read the proposal**, under the options, puts the discovery, goals, constraints, every option with what's in and out, how each price adds up, when you'd pay, purchases, what each of us will do, and the agreement on one page. **Print or save as PDF** keeps a copy, and **Download Word** gives one to mark up, each page carrying the number, such as 0039-r1. Approving in 1NEON counts the same as signing on paper. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### How do I suggest changes to a proposal?
Comment on an option, or mark up the Word download and upload it to **Files**. 1NEON asks "Is this a revision of Proposal 0039-r1?"; choose **Yes** and it's filed beside that revision. neon1 answers with the next revision. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Who buys equipment or software for a project?
You do, in your organization's name, unless you agree neon1 does. Each option lists expected purchases with estimates, which appear under **Purchases** in Execute once you approve; mark each bought and **Attach the receipt**. neon1 buys only with the decision maker's signature or initials, and is paid back at cost, with the receipt. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What if we need something new after approving?
Add it as a request. 1NEON asks whether it changes the agreed scope; if so, it waits for the next revision with its own price, so no work or cost is added without a written change. Anything else goes ahead as usual.

### Who can approve?
The project's named decision maker, or, if none, an admin of your organization. Only an admin can name or change the decision maker, under **People** on the overview, after confirming it's them, and each change is recorded. neon1 can't be named, since it proposes the options, except in its own organization. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Can I change my mind after approving an option?
Yes, until you accept the handover. The decision maker picks another option and presses **Choose this instead**, says why, and signs again. Before signing, the screen shows what the change replaces, what was already invoiced, the hours already logged, and the deposit asked now, if any. Nothing is billed twice: what was invoiced for the earlier option counts toward the new one, hours already worked count toward its hours, and if what you've paid comes to more than the new option can cost, the difference comes back as a credit at handover. The earlier decision isn't edited or deleted; it stays on record, marked replaced, with your reason on the new one. To go back to an option you replaced, neon1 confirms it in the next revision of the proposal, with today's prices, and you choose it again the same way. If the change needs work none of the options describe, ask neon1 for a new option. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### What does approving an option commit us to?
An approval records that your organization agreed to that option as shown: its scope, cost, timeline, and any conditions. Both sides can rely on it, alongside your signed agreement with neon1, which governs the paid work.

### How do I accept a handover step?
On the **Normalize** layer, steps are ticked as they're done. Once all are, the decision maker chooses **Accept the handover**, notes what they checked if they like, and signs with their typed name. One signature accepts every step, each showing who accepted it and when. **Check it and accept** accepts one step early. Accepted steps can't be edited, and acceptance prompts the final invoice. Watch [the handover](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=handover) and [the final invoice](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=final).

### What do Cost, Quality, and Effort mean?
neon1's 1 to 5 assessment of each option, where more dots is always better. **Cost** is the total cost, **Quality** how fully it meets your requirements, and **Effort** your team's effort to adopt and run it. The written price is what you'd agree to, with one-time and recurring charges apart, and **How this adds up** shows the hours and when you'd pay. Demo and walkthrough figures are illustrative. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=propose).

## Files

### How do I send a file?
Use **Upload** next to a file I asked for, or upload in **Files** and pick its project. Tap a name to open it: PDFs, pictures, and text open in a tab, and anything else downloads. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=listen).

### Are files safe?
Every file is virus-checked before anyone can download it and stays private to your organization. Please don't upload passwords or keys.

### What can I upload?
Any kind of file, up to 100 MB each. Please don't upload passwords, keys, or other secrets, malware, or regulated data such as health or payment card details unless we have a written agreement that covers it.

## People and access

### What can viewers, editors, and admins do?
Viewers read and comment. Editors also add and change work. Admins also invite people and change access and settings. neon1 sets its own access, so People shows it without a way to change it. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=team).

### How do I invite someone?
Admins choose **Invite people** on **People**, and the person gets an email link. People shows whether each invitation went out; if not, or it got lost, choose **Resend**. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=team).

### Can 1NEON text me?
Once texts are on, yes. Add your mobile number on your profile, agree to texts, save, and type the code 1NEON texts you. Texts only say something new is waiting, with a link, never naming your organization, projects, or files. Reply STOP to turn them off. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=texts).

### Why does it ask me to confirm it's me?
Admin actions (inviting, changing access or settings, and deleting a project or someone else's comment or file) need a recent confirmation with a passkey, authenticator app, or text code, so a stolen email account isn't enough. Add any of them under Two-step verification on your profile; a second is a backup. Once confirmed, you won't be asked again for 12 hours in that sign-in. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey).

### Can I use an authenticator app?
Yes. On your profile, choose **Set up** next to Authenticator app, scan the code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, or similar, and type the 6-digit code it shows. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=authenticator).

### What's a passkey?
A sign-in with your face, fingerprint, or device PIN instead of a code. It's faster and phishing-resistant, since it only works on the real 1NEON site. Add one under Two-step verification on your profile, and 1NEON offers it first next time. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey).

## Your account

### How do I change my name or the look?
Open your profile from the menu, change your name, title, theme, menu, or notifications, and choose **Save**. **Mini** keeps a slim menu of icons on the left. Two-step verification and signing out take effect at once. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=profile).

### How do I sign out?
Open your profile and choose **Sign out**.

### I lost my phone or my authenticator app.
You can still sign in with an emailed code; the second step is only for admin actions. If you have another method, confirm with it, then remove the lost one under Two-step verification and add a new one. If you've lost every method, email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech) from your account's address. Email alone isn't enough, since a stolen mailbox is what the second step guards against, so I first confirm who you are by calling a number already on record for you or your organization (never one from the email), plus another admin's written OK if your organization has one. Then I remove the lost methods, record it, and email you, and you add a new one. Meanwhile only admin actions wait. There's no self-service reset.

### My email address changed.
Your email is how 1NEON knows you, so it can't be changed on your profile. Ask an admin to invite the new address and deactivate the old one, or email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech). Earlier work stays under the old address.

### Who do I ask for access?
An admin of your organization. If you don't know who that is, or neon1's access needs to change, email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech).

## Invoices and paying

### Is a price fixed or an estimate?
The option says which, and **How this adds up** shows the work behind it. A fixed price is the whole price for the work described, changed only by a change you approve. An estimate is a range of hours at the option's hourly rate: you pay for hours worked, each listed as it's logged, never past the high end without your approval. Fewer hours cost less. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Where do I see the hours worked?
In **Hours**, under Execute. Each entry has its date, what it went to, and its length in quarter hours, under a bar showing hours against the estimate and their cost so far. Billed hours carry the invoice's number, and the invoice lists them, so the total can be checked. On a fixed price, hours are a record, not a bill. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What if the work needs more hours than the estimate?
The high end is the most you pay for the approved work; hours past it are listed, not billed. If the work itself must change, neon1 says so before the high end and issues a revision with the new price, and nothing is added until you approve. If the work takes less than the deposit covered, neon1 refunds the difference as a credit.

### Where are invoices and payments?
In **Invoices**, under Execute, with what's paid and left on each. **Next step** links to one that's ready or overdue. Each opens on neon1's letterhead with its number, lines, due date, and how to pay, ready to print or save as a PDF. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=pay).

### How do I pay an invoice?
Choose **Pay**. Stripe's hosted payment page opens for bank transfer (ACH, preferred) or card, and 1NEON never sees your details. A card shows as paid within a minute. A bank transfer shows as started that day and paid once it clears, usually in a few business days, with **Pay** gone meanwhile. Check, Zelle, or wire work too, with the invoice number in the memo, and neon1 records them on arrival. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=pay).

### What if my bank payment doesn't go through?
No money moved. The invoice reopens with the failed payment listed, 1NEON tells you and neon1 that day, and **Next step** and **Needs you** point to it. Choose **Pay again** or another way to pay. Nothing is added to what you owe.

### Do I get a receipt?
The invoice is your receipt. Once paid it reads **Paid in full**, with each payment's date, amount, and method, and prints or saves as a PDF. Billing contacts are also emailed when a payment arrives.

### Is there a fee for paying by card, or for paying late?
No. The total is what you pay, however you pay, with no card surcharge, late fee, or interest. A late invoice shows as overdue, and neon1 may pause work, after telling you, once one is more than 30 days overdue.

### What if I think an invoice is wrong?
Say so before you pay: email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech) with the invoice number and line. Invoices for hours list every entry, so you can point to one. An issued invoice is never edited; neon1 fixes it with a credit, or voids it with a reason and issues a new one, on the record.

### When are invoices sent?
As your option's **When you pay** says. A deposit, half a fixed price or half an estimate's low end, comes at approval, and the rest at handover acceptance: the remainder of a fixed price, or the hours worked less the deposit, listed. Purchases are billed at cost with the receipt, and care plans monthly. Invoices are due in 15 days unless the option says otherwise, and each emails your billing contacts. Watch [the deposit](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=pay) and [the final invoice](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=final).

### Can an invoice be changed?
Not once issued; it's kept exactly as issued. A mistake is fixed with a credit, or by voiding it with a reason, both on record. An invoice with a payment, or one clearing, can't be voided.

### What is the service agreement?
neon1's terms for all work with your organization: how work is agreed, paying, ownership, confidentiality, and ending it. An admin signs it once in 1NEON, from **Settings**, before the first approval, and it covers every project. Read it any time at [/1NEON/agreement](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/agreement). [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

## Support and your data

### Something urgent is wrong. Who do I tell?
Email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech) with what happened and when. neon1 works planned hours with no emergency response, so for something that can't wait, such as bank fraud or an outage, contact the provider responsible first. Report a security problem in the portal itself to [security@neon1.tech](mailto:security@neon1.tech).

### Can we get a copy of our data?
Yes, any time, without asking me. An admin of your organization opens **Settings** and chooses **Download everything** under Your data. It's one zip file: every project's records, decisions and signatures, proposals, invoices, payments, and handover steps as JSON and CSV (spreadsheets open the CSV files), plus every uploaded file as it was uploaded. A README in the zip says what each file holds.

### What happens to our data when the work ends?
It's kept as the record of our work together, so either of us can refer back to it, unless your organization asks me to delete it. Your organization can stop using 1NEON at any time, and can download everything from Settings first. The [privacy policy](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/privacy) has the details, including how long backups and audit records last.

### Who can I contact?
Email [hello@neon1.tech](mailto:hello@neon1.tech).
