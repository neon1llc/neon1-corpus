# The names

## neon1

The name nods to Neo from *The Matrix*, who brought peace between man and machine. Neo means new. Neon is unmissable. It's always written with a lowercase n.

## "1 is glad to be of service."

The slogan nods to Robin Williams' Andrew from *Bicentennial Man*, a machine who spends two centuries being of service while searching for his humanity. The 1 is whoever neon1 is working with.

## The legal names

- **neon1 LLC**, a New York limited liability company, does business as **neon1**.
- **N1 Supply LLC** does business as **N1 Supply**. It is an affiliate of neon1 LLC.

## Product names

Repositories that neon1 builds mainly for its own use carry the `neon1-` prefix, such as neon1-site and neon1-corpus. Products made for the public or for clients get names of their own, such as [Ready, Secure, Go](../products/ready-secure-go.md).
