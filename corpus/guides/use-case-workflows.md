# Use case: one workflow, no more retyping

## The situation

Requests arrive by web form, email, and phone. Someone copies each one into the scheduling or practice system, then into a spreadsheet to track follow-up. Things get missed or entered twice, and Monday mornings disappear into data entry.

## How it usually goes

1. **First call, 15 minutes.** We find the one workflow that costs the most and check that I'm the right fit.
2. **Discovery, a session or two.** I watch the real task, time a few examples, and write down what "fixed" looks like and what must not change.
3. **Proposal.** Options, from a better procedure to configuring what you already pay for to a small integration, each with its ongoing cost and a way out.
4. **Build, in small steps.** You see a working version early, with made-up data, before anything touches real records.
5. **Handover.** The person who does the work runs it alone, fixes a deliberate mistake, and keeps a one-page guide.
6. **Follow-up, a month later.** We compare missed and duplicate requests with the baseline.

## What usually changes

- Requests land once, in one place, and reach the system of record without retyping.
- Nothing waits unseen, because a daily summary lists anything unanswered.
- The tracking spreadsheet is retired, or kept as an export rather than a second copy.

## What usually doesn't

The tools you depend on usually stay. Often the fix is configuring what's already there and adding one small connection.
