# Intake, filled in

> [!example] A fictional example
> Fernwood Physical Therapy, its staff, and every detail below are invented to show what a finished document looks like. Any resemblance to a real practice is a coincidence. Prices are left out of public examples. A real proposal states them.

## You

**Business or household:** Fernwood Physical Therapy, a two-location outpatient practice

**Date:** March 3 **Decision maker:** Dana Reyes, owner and lead physical therapist

**Person who does the work day to day:** Sam Ortiz, front desk coordinator

**Best way to reach you:** Dana by email, Sam by phone between patients

## The problem

**In your words:** New patient requests come in through the website form, phone messages, and a shared inbox. Sam copies each one into the scheduling system and a spreadsheet. Some get missed, and some get entered twice.

**The last time it happened:** Last Tuesday a referral from a physician's office sat in the inbox for three days before anyone called the patient back.

**Current steps and tools:** Website form emails the shared inbox. Sam reads it, types the details into the practice management system, adds a row to a spreadsheet for follow-up, and calls the patient.

**How often, how long, and what it costs:** About 40 new requests a week, roughly 6 minutes each to retype, plus follow-up calls.
