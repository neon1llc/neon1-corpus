## Legal review of November 26, 2026, carried out

A legal review covered this file, the services agreement template, service agreement edition 2.3, the terms, the privacy policy, and the seven case studies. Its recommendations are now in the text:

- **engagement/service-agreement.md, edition 2.4.** Delivery, acceptance, and payment are separate. Invoices follow the approved billing schedule (estimated work every two weeks, fixed prices by milestone or on delivery), each sent within 7 days of what it bills and due in 15 days, and payment for properly performed work never depends on recorded acceptance. Paying doesn't mean accepting. A client disputes a named part of an invoice, and the undisputed amount stays due. Review is 10 business days after a recorded, notified handover, and silence never counts as acceptance. Client delays: notice, a possible pause after 10 business days, a possible ending after 30 calendar days, no charge for unperformed work. Reminder the business day after the due date, and a possible pause once an undisputed amount is more than 7 days overdue. Default deposit 30% of a fixed price or of an estimate's low end, adjustable by proposal. The defect period starts at actual handover. A proposal may change a section only by naming it. Liability: cap is the fees paid and payable for the project, with exceptions for gross negligence, willful misconduct, confidentiality, and what can't be limited, and payment owed is never capped. Permanent license to embedded neon1 tools, interim use before payment, and the client's systems, data, credentials, and records never withheld. Termination: what's payable, refunds, handover, and what survives. Forum (New York County, matching the terms) and a no-waiver-of-statutory-rights sentence. Signing records the organization's legal name and mailing address, the signer's authority, and the exact text, with a downloadable copy (Freelance Isn't Free Act and ESIGN).
- **Case studies.** No preselected choice: yes, decide later (ask about each project before publication), or no, chosen at signing. A definition of anonymized, separate yes for name, logo, quotations, endorsements, identifiable screenshots, and linking a name to a study, and withdrawal with removal from places neon1 controls. Edition 2.1 and 2.3 signers stay at decide later until an admin chooses; no earlier signature is turned into permission.
- **Earlier editions.** Approvals made under 2.1 or 2.3 keep those terms. New approvals need a 2.4 signature, so no new obligation applies to work already approved.
- **templates/services-agreement.md, edition 2.1.** Rewritten to match edition 2.4 clause for clause, with defaults filled in (30% deposit, 15 days, 7 days overdue, 14 days' notice) and no case-study box pre-marked.
- **policies/privacy.md, edition 4.0.** Chat disclosure (who reads ordinary and private messages, private isn't end-to-end encrypted, edit history kept, messages are project records, moderation and automatic mutes, network-address counters for two days, drafts in the browser). Seven-year retention stated as business practice, with New York's six-year contract period as the reason, plus a legal-hold row. Anonymized case studies allowed only as each client chose. The security limitation sentence is restored.
- **policies/terms.md, edition 1.8.** Chat conduct and moderation, and acceptance kept separate from payment.
- **templates/proposal.md, examples, 1neon/projects.md, faq/1neon-help.md, engagement/pricing.md, engagement/publishing-client-work.md.** Same terms in their own words.
- **Still open for a lawyer:** whether 30% and 7 days suit every engagement; the confidentiality term's length in the template (left blank); and the case-study evidence records listed in the owner's private claim register.
