# Partners

Some projects need another firm, vendor, or specialist alongside me. That works when everyone knows who's responsible for what, so these rules keep it written down.

## Before any shared work

A [partner responsibilities](../templates/partner-responsibilities.md) sheet is filled in and shared with the client. It names who sells, builds, supports, invoices, sees the data, owns the deliverables, and carries insurance, and what happens if the work ends.

## Fees and incentives

Any referral fee or benefit for recommending a vendor is disclosed before you decide. I don't recommend tools for affiliate programs, and I recommend free or built-in options when they fit.

## Accurate titles

- Working with an AWS Partner, Microsoft partner, or any other program member doesn't make neon1 one.
- A certification I hold personally doesn't certify the business.
- Building on a platform doesn't imply the platform endorses the work.
- Before naming a relationship or using a partner's logo, I check it's current and permitted.

## Vendors

Vendors bill you directly when possible, under your account, so you keep the relationship and data if neon1 steps away. I read their terms on data, export, and pricing before recommending them, and tell you what I found.

## Subcontracting

Your work and data go to no one else without your written approval, and with health information, any subcontractor signs the same business associate terms first.

## Referrals out

When someone else is better placed, I refer you to them, with no fee or obligation attached.

## Referrals in

Other firms are welcome to send me work that fits, such as a small business workflow or a security awareness session. The same rules apply in both directions.
