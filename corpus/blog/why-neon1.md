# Why I started neon1

The hard part of most technology work is rarely one system. It's the place where two or three of them meet: a point of sale that disagrees with a web store, a model that needs the right documents and the right permissions, a network that has to be segmented without breaking the equipment on it. Each piece usually has an owner. Few have one person looking at how they fit together.

I'm Pradeep. For more than eight years I've built and run cloud systems as an engineer, and the work I've learned the most from sat across those boundaries: integrations, migrations, AI that has to be measured, and networks that carry real equipment. neon1 is how I take that work on directly, for businesses, technical teams, and individuals.

I take on [four kinds of work](../engagement/index.md): workflow automation, cloud and AI engineering, networks and connected systems, and security readiness. I work remotely across the US, and on site within about 30 miles of Queens. Outside the US, ask.

Every project runs through [five steps](../engagement/process.md): listen, propose, decide, build, and hand over. I learn how the work gets done today, including what your own team already knows better than I do, before I suggest anything. Then you get one recommendation, or options when there's a real choice, and you decide. Sometimes the right option is a setting, a cleanup, or leaving something alone. When we're done, you have the documentation and the accounts, and everything stays yours.

This blog will carry about one post a week: answers to questions clients ask, like [what a federal security rule means for a tax office](tax-preparers-are-financial-institutions.md), why I run neon1 the way I do, and what building neon1 in the open has taught me.

## Starting is now free

Anyone can open a [free 1NEON account](../guides/free-accounts.md), the portal where I run every client project, and outline what their projects need in their own words. From the account you can ask for an assessment of up to three projects, or book a call, and I'll have read what you wrote before we talk.

The account holds up to 3 projects. Inviting your team, approvals, and billing open once we agree to work together. An account left unchanged for 30 days is removed after a few reminders. To see the portal first, the [guided walkthrough](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live) plays a made-up project in about three minutes.
