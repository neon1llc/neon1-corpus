# Free 1NEON accounts

You can start in [1NEON](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/), the portal my clients use, before we talk. Outline what your project needs, then ask for a free assessment of up to three projects, or book the free 30-minute call, whichever suits you. I read what you wrote before we talk. No card, no password.

Once you're in, **Contact neon1** in the menu has every way to reach me: email, call or text, or a booked call.

[Start a free account](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/?signup=1)

## How it goes

1. **Sign up** with your email, your name, and which describes you best (with your organization's name, if you have one). Your website or where you're based helps me, but it's optional. A code sent to your email finishes it.
2. **Outline a project.** Home's **Next step** and the project's **Overview** show six parts to write, such as what it is, one real example of how it works today, and what done looks like, with a tip for each part still missing. Each is optional, and [outlining a project](outline-a-project.md) shows what a good answer looks like. Each project's **Ready for Execute** percent shows how far along it is.
3. **Ask for an assessment** from **Next step** on Home: choose one to three projects and add a note, which is required. The assessment is free and covers those projects as you wrote them: I read them, then reply by email with a likely approach and the first questions to settle. It isn't a design, a proposal, or the paid [assessment](../engagement/index.md), which is a full written review with costed next steps. A project already sent waits 14 days before it can be sent again.
4. **We talk,** if you like, for 30 minutes about your needs. Book it from **Next step** or **Contact neon1** in the menu. You can book the call first, with or without an assessment. Options come afterward, in writing.
5. **If it fits,** we agree on the work, and your account becomes a client account. Everything you wrote stays, and the outline becomes the plan: its measure is tracked in Observe, and its people and tools shape the handover.

I work remotely across the US, and on site within about 30 miles of Queens, NY. Outside the US, ask: you're welcome to sign up or get in touch, and we'll see if it fits.

## What it holds

| | Free account | Client account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Projects | 3 | No set limit |
| Needs and notes in a project | 50 | No set limit |
| Inviting your team | Once we work together | Yes |
| Proposals, approvals, and invoices | Once we work together | Yes |
| Who sees it | You and me | Your team and me |
| Cost | Free | As agreed for each project |

**Get to know 1NEON** on Home links to a sample project, a short walkthrough of a free account, the demo, and the FAQs, so you can see the portal before you write anything. **Hide** it when you're done, and bring it back under Tutorial in your profile.

## How long it lasts

As long as you're using it. Home shows the date it's kept until. After 7 and 14 days without changes, you get a reminder by email. At 21 days you get a final notice, and at 30 days the account and what's in it are deleted. Need more time? **Keep it 30 more days** on Home (once), and **Download a copy** any time. You can sign up again any time.

## The fine print

Section 3 of the [terms of use](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/terms) covers free accounts, and the [privacy and data policy](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/privacy) covers what sign-up collects, including Cloudflare's invisible check that you're a person. I don't add you to a mailing list.
