# Free 1NEON accounts

You can start in [1NEON](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/), the portal my clients use, before we talk. Outline your project, then ask for an assessment of up to three projects, or book the first call. I read what you wrote before we talk. No card, no password. **Contact neon1** in the menu has every way to reach me.

[Start a free account](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/?signup=1)

## How it goes

1. **Sign up** with your email, your name, and which describes you best (with your organization's name, if you have one). Your website or location is optional. A code sent to your email finishes it.
2. **Outline a project.** Home's **Next step** and the project's **Overview** show six optional parts to write, with a tip for each one still missing. [Outlining a project](outline-a-project.md) shows what a good answer looks like. **Ready for Execute** shows how far along it is.
3. **Ask for an assessment** from **Next step** on Home: choose one to three projects and add a required note of a sentence or two. The assessment is free and covers those projects as you wrote them. I reply by email with a likely approach and the first questions to settle. It isn't a design, a proposal, or the paid [assessment](../engagement/index.md), a full written review with costed next steps. It goes to me 30 minutes after your last change, so you can edit or withdraw it until then, and send it again later.
4. **We talk,** if you like, for 30 minutes about your needs. Book it from **Next step**, ask me to reach out from there, or use **Contact neon1**. You can do this first. Options come afterward, in writing.
5. **If it fits,** we agree on the work, and your account becomes a client account. Everything you wrote stays, and the outline becomes the plan.

I work remotely across the US, and on site within about 30 miles of Queens, NY. Outside the US, ask. You're welcome to sign up, and we'll see if it fits.

| Proposals, approvals, and invoices | Once we work together | Yes |
| Who sees it | You and me | Your team and me |
| Cost | Free | As agreed for each project |

**Get to know 1NEON** on Home links to a sample project, a walkthrough, the demo, and the FAQs. **Hide** it when you're done, and bring it back under Tutorial in your profile.

## How long it lasts

As long as you're using it. Home shows the date it's kept until. While an outline is unfinished and nothing is waiting for me, you get outline tips by email about 3 and 7 days after your last change, then no more. Turn them off under Outline tips in your profile's Notifications. After 7 and 14 days without changes, you get a reminder. At 21 days you get a final notice, and at 30 days the account and what's in it are deleted. **Keep it 30 more days** on Home (once) if you need more time, and **Download a copy** any time. You can sign up again.

Section 3 of the [terms of use](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/terms) covers free accounts, and the [privacy and data policy](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/privacy) covers what sign-up collects, including Cloudflare's invisible check that you're a person. I don't add you to a mailing list.
