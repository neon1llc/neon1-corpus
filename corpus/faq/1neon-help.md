# FAQs

Short answers about using 1NEON. Signed in? Tap **?** at the top right of any page for help with that page.

## Getting started

### What is 1NEON?
The portal where you and I run your projects together. Each project keeps what you need, the work, the results, and the handover in one place. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live).

### Can I try it first?
Yes. [The demo](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/demo/) is a working copy of 1NEON with made-up clients. Pick who to be, and everything you change stays in your browser. [The example](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/example) walks one made-up project from start to finish, and [the live playback](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live) plays one out on screen, step by step, with controls to pause or jump between sections. Short ones show a single thing start to finish: [joining from an invitation](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite), [adding a passkey](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey), [adding an authenticator app](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=authenticator), and [texts and text codes](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=texts).

### How do I get in?
You need an invite. Open the link in your invite email, enter your email address, and type the code I send you. No password needed. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

### I didn't get the sign-in code.
Check your spam or promotions folder, then wait a minute and choose **Send a new code**. Codes work for a few minutes, in the browser where you asked for them. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

### Is there a tour?
Yes. It's offered the first time you sign in. Skip it or ask to be reminded next time, and replay it anytime from **?** at the top right or from your profile. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=invite).

## Projects and the four layers

### What are Needs, Execute, Observe, and Normalize?
The four layers every project moves through, N E O N. **Needs** is what you want and the options to get there. **Execute** is the work. **Observe** checks the results against the goals. **Normalize** makes the change last: handover, training, and care. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live).

### What should I do first on a project?
Look at **Next step** at the top of the project. It says what has to happen now and who it's waiting on, and it changes as soon as that's done: approve an option or send a file and it moves on to the next thing. When nothing is waiting on anyone, it names the stage's goal and offers **Move to** the next stage. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=propose).

### What do Not started, On track, At risk, and Off track mean?
They're the project's health. A new project is **Not started**, and it turns **On track** by itself once it moves past its first stage or any of its work is in progress or done. After that, the team sets it on the project's overview, or with a status update. Items have their own status in their panel: To do, In progress, Blocked, or Done. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What if we rename, reorder, or hide stages?
Projects follow along. Their steps, the board, and Next step use the new names and order. A hidden stage still shows while a project is in it. Decide keeps asking for an approved option, and Closed still ends a project, under any name or position.

### How do I tell you what I need?
Open the project's **Needs** tab and tap **Add request**. Finish a sentence with a few taps: what you'd like to do (choose any, or tap **+** to add your own), what it's for, what happens currently, how often it happens, and when it's due, including a date or a date range. Tap **Define another need** to add more. Each need becomes its own request, and only the first choice is required. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=needs).

### Do I have to write a lot?
No. Each section says what belongs there, shows an example, and offers suggestions you can add with one tap. Discovery questions take a short answer right where they're asked, and a real example helps most. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=start).

### How do I know when something changes?
The bell at the top glows softly when something new happens, like a file upload, a decision, or a project moving stage. Tap it to see what changed and who did it, then tap one to open it. Important changes also email you; choose which in your profile under **Notifications**. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=listen).

### How do I comment or change an item?
Tap the item's title. A panel opens with its status, owner, due date, and details, and a place to comment. Everyone in your organization sees the comments. Options and measures open the same way, with their cost and scores, or unit and target, there too. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### How do I check something off?
Tick the box beside it. Every item that can be finished has one, and the counts on each tab and section show how many are done. A choice between options counts as one, done once an option is approved, and a measure counts as done when it reaches its target. Status updates and decisions are records, so they don't count. Files you upload aren't counted either; a **File needed** is, and it's checked off when you send the file. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=handover).

### How do I delete something?
Use the trash button on the item, or **Delete** in its panel. Either one asks first. Approved options and decisions can't be deleted, since they're the record of what was agreed; options themselves come from neon1. Files have their own **Delete** for the person who uploaded them and admins.

### What does the onion next to a project mean?
It shows progress. Needs is the center ring and Normalize is the outside one; each ring fills as that layer's work gets done.

### What does the board show?
Every project as a card, in the stage it's in. Columns run left to right from the first call to care after handover. Drag a card to move it, or use its **Move to** menu on a phone. A closed project moves back the same way, or use **Reopen** on its page. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=build).

### What does the timeline show?
Under **Projects**, **Timeline** draws each project from the day it started to its target date, colored by its layer. The dashed line is today, the diamond is the target, the lighter part is time still ahead, and red stripes are time past the target. Each row also says its stage and how many days are left. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=care).

### How do I start a project?
Choose **New project**, answer a few short questions, and the project starts with a checklist that fits. You can change everything later. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=start).

### What is Coming up on Home?
This week and the next two as a strip of days. Each mark is something dated: a task's due date, a milestone, or a project's target. Below it, everything dated in the next six weeks is listed by Overdue, This week, Next week, and Later. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=signin).

### What is Insights?
Charts made from your organization's own data: open and overdue work, how often things finish on time, days to a decision, work finished per week, where projects sit across the four layers, and how healthy they are. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=observe).

## Decisions and approvals

### How do I approve an option?
Open the project's **Needs** layer, compare the options, and choose **Approve this option**. You can add conditions, such as "only after 6pm". The approval is recorded with your name, the time, and a copy of the option's terms (title, description, cost, and timeline) exactly as you saw them. Options come from neon1; to ask for a change, comment on the option. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Who can approve?
The person named as the project's decision maker. If nobody is named, an admin of your organization can. Only an admin of your organization can name or change the decision maker, under **People** on the project's overview, after confirming it's them; editors can change everyone else on the project. Each change is recorded with the old and new decision maker, and neon1 can't be named, since it proposes the options. (In neon1's own organization, where neon1 is the client, it can.) [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=decide).

### Can an approval be changed?
Not edited. Once approved, neither the decision nor the option it came from can be edited or removed. To change course, a new option is proposed and approved; the earlier decision stays on record, marked as replaced, so the history stays clear.

### What do Cost, Quality, and Effort mean?
A quick comparison of each option, in plain words. Lowest cost, best quality, and lightest effort are the better ends. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=propose).

## Files

### How do I send a file?
Use **Upload** next to a file I asked for, or go to **Files** and upload it there. Pick the project it belongs to. Tap a file's name to open it: PDFs, pictures, and text open in a new tab, and anything else downloads. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=listen).

### Are files safe?
Every file is checked for viruses before anyone can download it, and stays private to your organization. Please don't upload passwords or keys.

## People and access

### What can viewers, editors, and admins do?
Viewers read and comment. Editors also add and change work. Admins also invite people, change their access, and change settings. neon1's own access is set by neon1, so it shows on People without a way to change it. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=team).

### How do I invite someone?
Admins go to **People** and choose **Invite people**. The person gets an email with a link. People shows whether each invitation email went out; if one didn't, or it got lost, choose **Resend**. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=team).

### Can 1NEON text me?
Once texts are switched on, yes. Add your mobile number on your profile, agree to texts, save, then type the code 1NEON texts you. Texts only say that something new is waiting, with a link; they never name your organization, projects, or files. Reply STOP to any text to turn them off. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=texts).

### Why does it ask me to confirm it's me?
Inviting people, changing access, and changing settings need a second check, so a stolen email account isn't enough to make changes. On your profile, under Two-step verification, add a passkey, an authenticator app, or text-message codes, then confirm with it when asked. Any one works, and a second is a backup if you lose the first. Once confirmed, you won't be asked again for 12 hours in that sign-in. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey).

### Can I use an authenticator app?
Yes. On your profile, choose **Set up** next to Authenticator app, scan the code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, or similar, and type the 6-digit code it shows. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=authenticator).

### What's a passkey?
A sign-in that uses your face, fingerprint, or device PIN instead of a code. It's faster and can't be phished. Add one on your profile under Two-step verification; next time you sign in, 1NEON offers it first. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=passkey).

## Your account

### How do I change my name or the look?
Open your profile from the menu. You can change your name, title, theme, menu, and notifications there, then choose **Save**. The menu button (top left, and Menu at the bottom on phones) opens the full menu; choose **Mini** to also keep a slim menu of icons on the left. Two-step verification and signing out work right away. [Watch it](https://neon1.tech/1NEON/live?play=profile).

### How do I sign out?
Open your profile and choose **Sign out**.
